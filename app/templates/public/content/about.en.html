Human to Human. People for People.

Help Lagbe? is a secular, public-interest platform for people in Bangladesh who need information, a route to help, or a way to report wrongdoing. It is run by trained people. Every report is read by a human, every severity level is assigned by a human with written reasoning, and every live conversation is with a human.

What we do

  • Receive reports, anonymously or confidentially, and help people reach services that can act.
  • Provide plain information on personal safety, legal processes and digital security.
  • Publish awareness material and, after careful verification and with consent, reported findings in the public interest.

What we do not do

  • We do not dispatch emergency services.
  • We do not provide legal representation or medical care.
  • We do not declare anyone guilty.
  • We do not contact anyone on your behalf without your explicit request.

How we are accountable

Our rules are public: safeguarding, privacy, editorial policy, corrections, complaints and accessibility. Access to confidential identity records is logged and reviewed. Every publication is checked by a second person before approval. Corrections are listed publicly. If you have a complaint about the platform itself, including about a member of staff, you can make it through the request form and it will not be handled by the person it concerns.
