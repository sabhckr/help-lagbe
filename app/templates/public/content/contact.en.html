How to reach us

This platform does not publish a general phone number, email address or office address. That is deliberate: it keeps every conversation inside channels that are logged, reviewed and designed to protect you. Use the channel that matches what you need.

  • To report something or ask for help: the report form.
  • To follow up on a case: Track My Case, with your reference and one-time key.
  • To talk to a person now: Live Human Support, when an agent is online.
  • To request a correction, reply, appeal or removal, or to complain: the request form.

What to expect

Whichever channel you use, a trained person reads what you send; there are no bots. We never contact a family member, employer or alleged perpetrator unless you explicitly ask, and we do not dispatch emergency services.

Emergency and helpline numbers

Verified numbers for emergency services and for helplines run by other organisations appear in the box on this page. We do not operate those services and cannot dispatch them.

For staff

Trained workers sign in at the staff login. Members of the public never need an account to report, track a case or use live support.
