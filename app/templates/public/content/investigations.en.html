This page lists stories and investigations we have published in the public interest, together with case outcomes that have been formally verified. Nothing appears here because a report was submitted. Each piece has passed consent and safety review, source and evidence assessment, redaction, review by a second person, legal review where needed, and approval. Right of response was offered before any allegation about an identifiable person or institution was published.

How to read the labels

Each piece is labelled reported allegation (a claim made to us, not independently established), corroborated finding (supported by evidence we examined ourselves), official statement, court-established outcome or awareness material. A label describes the strength of a claim, not anyone's guilt. Verified outcomes shown below are recorded only when an official result has been confirmed. Our editorial policy explains each step.

If you are named in a piece or affected by one, you can request a reply, a correction or a removal.
