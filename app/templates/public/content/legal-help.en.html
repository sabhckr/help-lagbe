Information and pathways, not legal advice

Help Lagbe? can explain how a process usually works, what your options may be, and which kinds of service exist for your kind of problem. It cannot give you legal advice about your particular situation, and it cannot represent you. Nothing on this page, and nothing a worker says to you through this platform, is legal advice.

How legal questions are handled

When you submit a case with a legal question, or a worker sees that a case has a legal dimension, it goes to the platform's legal review. Qualified lawyers review legal matters before any information is passed back to you. They may ask you for details through Track My Case. Where appropriate, they will point you to a service that can advise or represent you.

Deadlines matter

Many legal remedies have time limits. Waiting can close doors that were open. If you think a deadline may apply to you, say so in your report and seek qualified advice as early as you can, even while your case with us is still in review.

Legal aid and verified contacts

Free or low-cost legal aid may exist for your situation. Verified contacts for legal aid and related services appear in the box on this page. We check them before listing them. We do not list named lawyers, and we do not recommend individuals.

Keep your records

Keep copies of documents, messages, receipts and dates. Keep the originals safe. If you attach evidence to a report, keep your own copy as well.
