If you are in immediate danger

This platform does not dispatch police, ambulances, fire services or rescue teams. If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, contact the emergency numbers shown in the verified contacts box on this page, if you can do so safely. If a call would put you at greater risk, wait until you are somewhere safer.

What this platform can do

You can use Help Lagbe? to describe a problem, ask for information, or report something that has happened to you or to someone you know. A trained person reads what you send. Depending on what you need, they may explain your options, share verified information, point you towards a service that can act, or, with your agreement, pass your case to a specialist team. Nobody here replaces emergency services, a doctor or a lawyer.

Ways to reach us

  • Report form. Describe what happened in your own words, at your own pace. Evidence is optional. Open the report form.
  • Track My Case. After you submit, you receive a reference and a one-time key, shown once. Keep them safe. They let you return to see progress, read messages from the worker handling your case, and reply, without telling us who you are. Track my case.
  • Live Human Support. A text chat with a trained human agent, open only when an agent has set themselves available. When nobody is available, the page says so. There are no bots and no automated counsellors. Check whether support is open.

Helpline numbers run by other services appear in the box on this page. We check each one before listing it and remove any we can no longer verify.

Anonymous or confidential: you choose

An anonymous report collects no identity at all. Staff cannot contact you except through the Track My Case page, so if you want updates you must return there yourself. A confidential report lets you leave a name, phone number, email or safe-contact instructions. Those details are stored separately from your account of events, encrypted, and can only be opened by an assigned worker who records a written reason; every access is logged and reviewed. This protection is strong but not absolute: a court order, a serious risk to someone's life, or a duty to protect a child could require disclosure. We will not promise you otherwise. Read more on our privacy page.

What happens after you submit

  1. Submitted. Your report is received and given a reference.
  2. Triage. A trained person reads it and assigns a severity level with written reasoning.
  3. Assigned. A worker takes responsibility for the case.
  4. Under review. The worker studies the details and may ask you questions through Track My Case.
  5. Specialist review. Where needed, a legal, digital-safety or safeguarding specialist looks at the case.
  6. Referral or action. With your agreement, we refer you to a service that can act, or take platform action where we can.
  7. Follow-up. We check what happened next.
  8. Resolved or closed. The case is closed with the outcome recorded. You can appeal, and a closed case can be reopened.

We record outcomes truthfully, in plain terms: referral offered, complaint submitted with confirmation, platform action confirmed, official outcome verified, investigation ongoing, or outcome unknown. We do not claim results we cannot show.

What we will never do without your request

We never contact a family member, an employer or the person you have reported unless you explicitly ask. We will not advise you to confront anyone. You decide what happens next, and you can stop at any point.
