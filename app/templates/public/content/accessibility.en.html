What we have built in

  • Keyboard operation. Everything can be reached and used with a keyboard, and the element in focus is visibly outlined.
  • Two languages. A switcher lets you choose English or Bengali on any page.
  • Bengali text. Pages use fonts chosen to render Bengali script correctly, including conjunct letters.
  • Reduced motion. If your device asks for reduced motion, animation is turned off.
  • Text resizing. You can enlarge text with your browser's zoom without breaking the layout.
  • Screen readers. Controls, form fields and status messages carry labels for screen readers.
  • No time limits. Forms do not expire while you are filling them in.

Leaving the site quickly

A Quick exit button is on every page. Pressing it, or pressing the Esc key three times, ends your tracking or chat session and takes you to a neutral website. It does not clear your browser history; our digital safety page explains how to do that.

Tell us about a barrier

If something stops you using the site, we want to know. There is no separate accessibility channel; use the request form with the type complaint: report a barrier. Describe what you were trying to do, what happened, and which device, browser or assistive technology you were using. These reports are read by a person and used to fix the site.
