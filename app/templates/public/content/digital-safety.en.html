Protect your accounts

  • Use a strong, unique password for every account. Do not reuse one password across accounts.
  • Turn on two-step verification wherever it is offered.
  • Check which devices are signed in to your accounts and sign out any you do not recognise.

Recognise phishing

Messages that create urgency, ask for a code, a password or a payment, or lead to a login page that looks slightly wrong are warning signs. Do not tap links in unexpected messages. Open the service yourself and check there.

If you are being threatened or blackmailed

  • Do not pay. Paying rarely ends it and usually brings more demands.
  • Do not delete the conversation. Take screenshots that show the date, the time and the account name of the sender.
  • Stop replying once you have preserved the evidence.
  • Report the account to the platform it is on, and keep the report reference.
  • You can report it here as well. We can explain your options and refer you to services that act. We cannot remove content ourselves and we do not dispatch police.

Browsing privately

If someone else can see your device, use private browsing, clear your history after visiting sensitive pages, and use the Quick exit button on this site, or press Esc three times, to leave quickly. Check your phone's settings for location sharing and for apps you did not install.

Verified contacts for digital-safety services appear in the box on this page. Report online harassment or account misuse.
