Below you will find awareness material published by this platform: plain explanations of common situations, the warning signs, and what you can do about them. These pieces are general. They do not describe any individual case, and they never include details that could identify a person who reported to us.

How to read the labels

Every item carries a label. Awareness material is general information. Where an item quotes an authority, that part is labelled official statement. Where it draws on a case we have looked into, the relevant part is labelled reported allegation, corroborated finding or court-established outcome, as explained in our editorial policy. A label describes the strength of a claim, not anyone's guilt.

If something here is wrong or out of date, you can request a correction. If you recognise a situation from your own life, the get help page explains what you can do next.
