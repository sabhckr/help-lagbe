Complaining about us

If you are unhappy with how this platform has treated you, you can complain. That includes the conduct of a worker, the way your case was handled, a decision we made, something we published, or a barrier that stopped you using the site.

How to complain

Use the request form and choose the type complaint: make a complaint. Tell us what happened, when, and what you would like to see changed. If it concerns a case, include your case reference; you do not have to reveal your identity. You may leave a contact so that we can reply; it is stored encrypted and used only for the complaint.

How complaints are handled

  • A complaint about a staff member is never handled by that staff member, and they are not shown your identity.
  • Complaints are read by a person with responsibility for conduct, who records what was found and what was done.
  • If you left a contact, you receive the answer through it. If you did not, we cannot reply to you directly, but the outcome is still recorded.
  • If you disagree with the answer, you can ask for an appeal, which is reviewed by a different person.

What a complaint does not do

Making a complaint does not affect how your case is handled, and you will not be treated differently for complaining. A complaint is not a way to reach emergency services; if someone is in danger, use the emergency numbers in the verified contacts box on this page.
