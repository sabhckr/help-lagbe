Our approach

We collect as little as we can, keep what we collect separate and protected, and tell you plainly where the limits are. This page describes what actually happens, not what we would like to promise.

What we collect depends on your choice

Anonymous report. We collect no identity: no name, no phone number, no email, no account. Staff cannot contact you. The only way to communicate is the Track My Case page, which you open with the reference and one-time key shown once after you submit. If you lose them, we cannot recover your case for you, because we have no way to know it is yours.

Confidential identified report. You may give a name, phone number, email address and instructions for contacting you safely. These are stored separately from the narrative of your case and encrypted. The workers who read your case see the narrative, not your identity. Only an assigned worker who records a written reason can open the identity record. Every access is logged, and the log is reviewed.

Live Human Support. You do not need to give a name to chat. What you write is handled like a report.

Requests. If you ask for a correction, reply, appeal or removal, or make a complaint, you may leave a contact so we can respond. It is stored encrypted and used only for that request.

Evidence you upload

Files you attach are stored with your case and are visible only to the workers handling it. Sensitive material such as intimate imagery or identity documents is never published and is handled under additional restrictions. Keep your own copies.

Where confidentiality ends

Our protection is restricted, not absolute. A court order, a serious risk to someone's life, or a duty to protect a child could require us to disclose information. If that ever happens, a person decides, writes down the reason, and discloses only what is necessary. We will not tell you that this can never happen.

What we never do with your information

  • We never contact a family member, employer or alleged perpetrator unless you explicitly ask.
  • We never sell your information or share it for marketing.
  • We never publish a child's identifying details, private addresses, phone numbers, identity documents, intimate imagery or sensitive evidence.
  • We never use AI to talk to you in support or to decide your case.

Your choices and requests

You can appeal a decision, ask for a case to be reopened, or ask for your information or a publication to be removed. Use the request form: removal, appeal, correction. A removal request is weighed against safeguarding duties and any review that is still open. If you left a contact, you receive the answer through it; if you did not, we cannot reply to you directly, but the decision is still recorded.

The website itself

The site stores your language choice and, while you use Track My Case or live chat, a temporary session. The Quick exit button, or pressing Esc three times, ends those sessions and takes you to a neutral site. Pages that hold personal information are not saved for offline use. Reports and requests are rate-limited to protect the service. Staff logins and access to confidential records are logged.
