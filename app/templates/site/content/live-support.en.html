Who you will talk to

Live Human Support is a text chat with a trained human agent. There are no bots, no automated replies and no AI counsellors. We never use AI to talk to people in support. If you are chatting, a person is reading.

When it is open

The chat is open only when a trained agent has set themselves available and the service is marked as staffed. When nobody is available, this page says it is closed. We do not show a queue that nobody is watching. The current status is shown above.

What the chat is for

Agents can listen, explain your options, help you decide whether to submit a report, and point you to verified services. They do not give legal or medical advice, cannot dispatch police, ambulances or rescue, and cannot act on your behalf outside the platform. If you are in immediate danger, use the emergency numbers in the verified contacts box on this page, if you can do so safely.

Privacy of the chat

You do not have to give your name. The conversation is handled under the same rules as a report, described on our privacy page. Agents never contact a family member, employer or alleged perpetrator unless you explicitly ask. Use the Quick exit button, or press Esc three times, if you need to leave the site immediately.

If the chat is closed

You can still submit a report at any time, or return to Track My Case. See all the ways to get help.
